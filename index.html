About the Host

Jonathan W. Buckley

As a kid, my idols weren't rock stars; they were inventors and founders. Buying my first Mac in 1985 sparked a lifelong obsession.

As founder and CEO of The Artesian Network, a boutique Fractional GTM team, I've helped commercialize early-stage startups — more than half reaching IPO or acquisition. This podcast is where I go deeper: not to collect polished success stories, but to document how founders actually build.

Where did the idea come from? What forced the leap from curiosity to commitment? How did they determine what counted as a true MVP? How did the first money and first customers happen — and what did it take to stay in the fight?

"The product design process, no matter how sophisticated the product, should drive towards 'Fisher-Price Simplicity'."
