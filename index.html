About the Host

Jonathan W. Buckley

Jonathan W. Buckley is an enterprise tech strategist, fractional CEO & CMO, and founder of The Artesian Network — a boutique fractional marketing team helping early-stage B2B tech companies position, launch, and scale faster.

He has served as CMO and VP of Marketing for tech companies both public and private, from Silicon Valley to the Midwest. As the creator of We Built It Because We Had To, Jonathan goes beyond the highlight reel to document how founders actually build: where ideas come from, what forces commitment, and what it truly takes to find repeatable revenue.

"The product design process, no matter how sophisticated the product, should drive towards 'Fisher-Price Simplicity'."

Jonathan also serves as a strategic advisor to founders and boards, helping companies navigate product-market fit, messaging strategy, and growth challenges. He recently delivered guest lectures at Ohio State University's ECE department on startup growth and revenue engineering.
